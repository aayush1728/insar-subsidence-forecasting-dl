   advanced InSAR and DGPS — International Journal of Digital Earth, 2024.
   https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/19475705.2024.2375546

   - Reports Korba rate: ~-21 mm/yr LOS (Monika et al., 2015-2017)
